# Who announces it, who hears, and where the gaps are

Regulators grade recalls by the likelihood of harm, and the grade determines how urgently and widely it is communicated.

## Recalls are classified by severity

Regulators grade recalls by the likelihood of harm, and the grade determines how urgently and widely it is communicated. The classification is published with the notice and is the first thing to read, because it sets everything else.

## The notice is a document with structure

A recall notice names the product, the identifying batch or lot numbers, the reason, the hazard and the action required. Anything circulating without those fields is a report of a recall rather than the recall itself.

## Batch numbers are the whole point

Recalls are almost always limited to specific production runs. A reader cannot tell whether a notice applies to what they hold without the batch identifier, which is why that number belongs on the product and on the documentation.

## Notification reaches sellers before buyers

Distribution chains are notified directly; individual purchasers usually are not, unless a seller holds contact details and chooses to use them. The gap between a recall being issued and a buyer learning of it is the main weakness of the system.

## Registries are searchable

Regulators maintain public, searchable recall databases covering years of notices. Checking a product against one takes a minute and is the only route that does not depend on a seller passing the information on.

## A recall is not an admission of general fault

Many are precautionary, triggered by a labelling error or a single out-of-specification test. Treating every recall as evidence of systemic failure misreads the mechanism, which is designed to be used early.

## Absence of a recall proves little

Products outside a regulated category may have no mechanism through which a recall could be issued. No notices found is therefore a weaker statement for some categories than others, and the distinction is rarely made.

## What a careful seller does

Publishes batch identifiers, retains contact details, and notifies buyers directly when a notice affects a run they received. Each is checkable before ordering.

## Checking a batch against a registry

Regulator databases are searchable by product and by batch identifier and cover years of notices. The check takes about a minute and is the only route that does not depend on a seller choosing to pass information along.

## Why severity grading matters most

The classification determines how widely a notice is pushed and how quickly. A low-grade recall may never reach a buyer through any channel other than a database search, which is the main practical argument for searching rather than waiting.
