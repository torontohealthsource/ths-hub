## Use it as the measuring stick

The practical value of listing it alongside the rest is contrast. When a summary says a compound is 'well researched', the question is whether it means this — registered phase three trials and a post-market record — or a dozen rodent papers. Both get described the same way.

## Approval attaches to a product, not a molecule

What regulators approved is a specific manufacturer's formulation at defined doses. Material of the same molecule from a research supplier is outside that, and the published safety record does not transfer to it. This is the single most common error made about this compound.
