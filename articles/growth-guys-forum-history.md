### Does a long forum history mean a vendor is safe?

No. It means there has been a long period in which problems could have surfaced publicly and did not. That is evidence about exposure, not about product quality, and it should be weighed alongside current lab reports rather than instead of them.

### Is a forum thread better evidence than reviews on the vendor's own site?
