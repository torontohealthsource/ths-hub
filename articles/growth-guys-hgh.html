The price on the page is not the price you are comparing

HGH is listed by kit and assayed by vial, so the number on the listing and the number on the report describe different objects. Any per-unit comparison that mixes them is wrong before the arithmetic starts.

This is the single most common reading error on this product, and it runs in the alarming direction: it makes a correctly filled kit look badly underfilled.

The trap, stated plainly

A kit's product name states the total for the kit. The laboratory report covers one vial out of that kit. Divide the report's mass by the kit total and every kit in the set appears to hold roughly a tenth of its labelled strength.

That tenth is the vial count, not a shortfall. The six published kit reports are all consistent with ten vials per kit, which is the denominator the arithmetic needs.

The six published kit reports, read correctly

Read per vial, against one tenth of the labelled kit total, the six reports land between 97.2% and 109.4% of label: 110iu kit 11.03mg against 11.0 (100.3%), 400iu kit 38.88 against 40.0 (97.2%), 150iu kit 15.93 against 15.0 (106.2%), 270iu kit 27.65 against 27.0 (102.4%), 450iu kit 44.90 against 45.0 (99.8%), and a second 400iu kit 43.77 against 40.0 (109.4%).

Four of the six are above label rather than below it. Overfill is the ordinary direction for this product, because a filler who undershoots produces a complaint and one who overshoots does not.

Purity on these kits runs lower than the rest of the catalogue, and that is expected

The six kit reports carry purity figures from 95.88% to 97.55%, batches GGC28 through GGC33. Against a catalogue average of 99.03% that looks like a weak result, and read without context it would be.

It is not the same measurement. HGH is a 191-amino-acid protein; the single peptides that make up most of the catalogue are short chains. A long protein carries related-substance peaks that a short chain does not, so a high-90s figure here is not comparable to a high-99s figure on a nine-amino-acid peptide. Compare like with like or the number misleads.
