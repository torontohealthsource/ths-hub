Why this question gets asked at all

The worry behind it is reversibility. A card payment can usually be disputed through the issuer; a cryptocurrency transfer generally cannot. A buyer asking whether a seller takes only one of those is really asking what recourse exists if the order goes wrong, which is a reasonable thing to want to know before paying.

It is worth separating that from a second, weaker inference people often draw: that a seller accepting cryptocurrency is somehow less legitimate. In this category, payment options are shaped mostly by which processors will underwrite the merchant category at all, and that is a banking decision rather than a statement about the goods.
