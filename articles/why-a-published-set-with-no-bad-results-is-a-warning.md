### Why would perfect lab results be a bad sign?

Because real measurement varies. Different compounds, synthesis runs and storage conditions produce a spread of purity figures. A set where every published result sits above 99% has more often been filtered than achieved, and filtering cannot be detected from inside the set.

### Does a missing lab report mean a product is untested?

Not necessarily. Testing lags production, so a recently added item can be untested-so-far rather than deliberately untested. Judge the coverage pattern across the whole range rather than any single gap.
