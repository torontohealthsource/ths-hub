The search "growth guys canada scam" is really two questions

One is commercial: does the seller take money and fail to deliver, or refuse to resolve a concrete order problem. That is a question about conduct, and the people best placed to answer it are buyers describing a specific order with dates.

The other is about material: is the contents of the vial what the label says. That is a question about testing, and no amount of delivery satisfaction answers it. A parcel can arrive quickly, in good condition, with the right label, and still tell you nothing about what is inside.

Why forum threads rarely settle either one

A thread mixes the two freely. Somebody reports a slow parcel, somebody else replies that the product felt weak, and the conversation counts both as evidence for the same verdict. They are not evidence for the same verdict, and stacking them produces confidence without information.

Threads are also weak on identity. Names in this category are near-duplicates of each other, and a complaint about a similarly-named seller reads as a complaint about this one unless somebody checks the domain. If a thread does not state the exact site, it has not established who it is about.
