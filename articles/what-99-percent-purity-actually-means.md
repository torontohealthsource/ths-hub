### Does 99% purity mean the vial holds 99% of the labelled dose?

No. Purity is an area percentage describing what proportion of the detected material was the target compound. How much material is in the vial is a separate content or mass test. Of the 240 published reports, 178 carry a purity percentage and 62 are identity or content tests.
