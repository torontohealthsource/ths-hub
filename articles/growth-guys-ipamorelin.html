Why this compound is the fairest test of a published record

Ipamorelin is a short, well-characterised pentapeptide and one of the easiest things in this catalogue to assay cleanly. That makes its published figures more informative than they look.

A strong result on something easy proves less than a strong result on something difficult. But a weak result on something easy is a genuine signal, because there is no analytical excuse for it. That asymmetry is why this is the line to check first when judging a vendor's record.
