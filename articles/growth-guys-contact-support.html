Two kinds of question, two different places to ask

Order questions belong with support: where a parcel is, why a charge looks wrong, whether an address can still be changed, whether something arrived damaged. These are facts the seller holds and nobody else does, so support is the only place they can be answered.

Product-identity questions do not belong there, not because support would refuse but because an emailed assurance is the weakest form of the answer. Anything a seller tells you about its own material is asserted, and the whole value of a third-party testing record is that it is not.

Find the channel on the site, not in a forum thread

Contact details change and old threads preserve dead addresses, which is how people end up concluding a seller has gone quiet when they have simply written to an address that was retired. The live site is the only current source.

Why limited corporate detail is weak evidence either way

Suppliers in this category commonly publish little formal corporate information, and that observation is accurate. It is also close to useless as a test, because a founding date and a street address cost nothing to write and cannot be confirmed from outside without a records search most buyers will never run.
