### Why do coupon sites list codes from a different business?

Because those pages are assembled by matching brand names rather than by testing. The listing is usually accurate about some company — just not the one the reader is buying from.

### How do I know which business a discount code belongs to?

Check the domain the listing links to, not the brand name shown, and check whether the products described match what you are buying. A discount on coaching packages and a sitewide code at a compound vendor are different offers.
