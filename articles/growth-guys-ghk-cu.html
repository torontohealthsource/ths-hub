A copper peptide is not assayed like a plain peptide

GHK-Cu is a tripeptide bound to a copper ion, and that bond changes what a purity number is measuring. The analysis has to account for the metal complex, not just the peptide chain, so a figure here is not interchangeable with a figure on an uncomplexed peptide of similar length.

Readers searching this compound by name are usually past the general question and into this specific one, which is why a page that glosses it is no use to them.
