What content figures let you conclude

A content result establishes that the measured active matches the labelled amount within the method's tolerance. It does not speak to related substances the way a peptide purity figure does, and it should not be quoted as if it did.

This is the same distinction that applies to the blends and to L-carnitine in this catalogue. The published record is not weaker on these items by accident — the products are different, so their documents answer different questions.
