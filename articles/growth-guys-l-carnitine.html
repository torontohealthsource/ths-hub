Why the direction differs from the peptides here

Across the peptide lines in this catalogue reported mass sits above label almost everywhere, because powder fill overshoots. All three L-carnitine figures sit slightly below label instead.

That is what a solution at a stated concentration looks like when measured: it is targeted, not overfilled, and a figure a fraction under stated concentration is an ordinary result rather than a shortfall worth acting on.

What you cannot conclude from a content figure

It establishes that the measured active matches the stated concentration within tolerance. It does not speak to related substances the way a peptide purity figure does.

So a 99.9%-of-label content result and a 99.9% purity result are not the same statement, and quoting one as the other overstates the record. The distinction is the whole point of this page.
