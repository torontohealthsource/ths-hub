### Does a 99% purity result mean a product is sterile?

No. Purity and sterility are measured by different assays. A purity figure describes the proportion of stated compound and says nothing about bacterial residues.

### If a batch has no endotoxin result, did it fail one?

No conclusion is available either way. An absent assay means the question was not asked for that batch, which is different from a result that was obtained and withheld.
